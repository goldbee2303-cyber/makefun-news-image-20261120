Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

A dense newspaper-style image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026 — 16 stories on one page: a navy masthead with the date, a "THE DAY IN UTC" timeline, four illustrated lead stories, eight cards, a numbered briefs strip, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" map and a "DATE CHECK" footer.

Leads: Russia's record overnight barrage hits Kyiv's bridges and spills into Poland; Cyclone Akvan makes landfall near Gopalpur with 300,000 evacuated; the second EU–UK summit agrees trade, emissions and youth-visa deals; Asian stocks plunge in the AI sell-off, the Nikkei down ~5%. Cards: COP31 talks spill into overtime with no deal; Estonia closes its border with Russia; Chancellor Merz rejects a new Yalta; Ukraine's allies meet in Berlin; 34 suspected gang members killed in Haiti; Iraqi forces enter the Kataib Hezbollah stronghold; the US and Venezuela sign a military cooperation deal; young people in 25+ countries strike over living costs and militarization. Briefs: Hormuz tanker attacks intensify; dozens missing after a migrant boat capsizes off Senegal; a drone strike kills 10 at a gold mine in Sudan's South Kordofan; Bukele registers for a third term in El Salvador.

Built in the dense front-page style of the 19 November entry — timeline strip, detailed flat illustrations per story, numbered briefs, world map and date check — using only verified facts. The image is drawn as a front page and the program copies it to the output directory unchanged.

Run it with `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`. The image is 1024×1024 PNG.
